# Pre/Post-Evaluation Satisfaction Questionnaire

We sincerely thank you for your participation in our experiment. This form is designed to evaluate several aspects of the annotation task: confidence in the annotation process, the relevance of question detail and response options, the quality of data to ensure reliable annotations, as well as the quality and completeness of the evaluation criteria in the annotation tasks.

We ask that you complete this form **after your first five annotations**.

Thank you in advance for your contribution!
