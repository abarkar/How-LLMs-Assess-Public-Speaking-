# Expert Criteria and Subjective Dimension Questionnaire

The following interface was used for the annotation process, where public speaking coaches evaluated various aspects of speech transcriptions. Each question provided multiple-choice or scale options to ensure structured and consistent feedback.  

*Note: The original questionnaire was written in French and has been translated into English for documentation purposes.*

---

## Criteria Questions (A–C options)

1. **Topic Presentation**  
   *In your opinion, how would you evaluate the topic presentation?*

   **Options:**
   - **A:** The problem related to the topic is poorly explained.  
   - **B:** Acceptable explanation of the problem related to the topic.  
   - **C:** Very good explanation of the problem related to the topic.

---

2. **Structure**  
   *In your opinion, how well-structured is the speech in this transcription?*

   **Options:**
   - **A:** Difficulty in understanding key points; speech lacks argumentation.  
   - **B:** Key points are present but with incomplete reasoning.  
   - **C:** Key points are well-emphasised and supported with precise and relevant arguments.

---

3. **Language Level**  
   *In your opinion, how appropriate is the language level of the speech in this transcription?*

   **Options:**
   - **A:** Extensive use of jargon, slang, or limited vocabulary.  
   - **B:** Adequate language with sufficient vocabulary for precision.  
   - **C:** Appropriate language with a wide, engaging, and lively vocabulary.

---

4. **Passive Voice**  
   *In your opinion, how would you evaluate the use of passive voice in the speech?*

   **Options:**
   - **A:** Not a good balance – too little use of the passive voice and too much use of the active voice (e.g., the text comes across as too personal and not objective enough).  
   - **B:** A good balance between passive and active voice (e.g., the text gives the impression of objective, clear, but friendly and not impersonal speech).  
   - **C:** Poor balance – excessive use of the passive voice (e.g., the text gives the impression of impersonal speech, with long, incomprehensible sentences).

---

5. **Conciseness**  
   *How would you evaluate the sentence length in this transcription?*

   **Options:**
   - **A:** Short sentences make the text unclear.  
   - **B:** Sufficient sentence length to make the text clear.  
   - **C:** Excessively long sentences make the text unclear.

---

6. **Redundancy**  
   *In your opinion, what is the level of redundancy in the content of this transcription?*

   **Options:**
   - **A:** Little redundancy, making the text less clear.  
   - **B:** Content redundancy improves clarity.  
   - **C:** Excessive redundancy, making the text less clear.

---

7. **Negative Language**  
   *How would you evaluate the use of negative words/expressions in the speech?*

   **Options:**
   - **A:** No significant use of negative words.  
   - **B:** Noticed but does not give a negative impression.  
   - **C:** Noticed, giving a bad impression.

---

8. **Metaphor**  
   *How would you evaluate the use of metaphors in the speech?*

   **Options:**
   - **A:** No use of metaphors.  
   - **B:** Metaphor present but adds no significant value.  
   - **C:** Metaphor enhances and facilitates explanation.

---

9. **Storytelling**  
   *In your opinion, how would you evaluate the use of a story/personal example in the speech?*

   **Options:**
   - **A:** No story/personal example.  
   - **B:** Story present but adds little value.  
   - **C:** Relevant story, making the speech engaging.

---

## Likert-Scale Criteria

10. **Introduction**  
    *On a scale from 1 = not at all to 5 = completely, how good is the introduction, i.e., does it occupy optimal space and follow an effective organisational pattern? Does it orient the audience/reader to the topic and the speaker/author?*

    **Scale:** 1 (not at all) – 5 (very much)

---

11. **Conclusion**  
    *On a scale from 1 = not at all to 5 = completely, how good is the conclusion, i.e., does it occupy optimal space and follow an effective organisational pattern? Does it reinforce the thesis and provide psychological closure, while opening the door to real or similar cases?*

    **Scale:** 1 (not at all) – 5 (very much)

---

12. **Persuasiveness**  
    *On a scale from 1 = not at all to 5 = very much, how persuasive is the speech, i.e., does it effectively communicate a convincing message? Is the reasoning rigorous?*

    **Scale:** 1 (not at all) – 5 (very much)

---

13. **Clarity of Language**  
    *On a scale from 1 = not at all to 5 = very much, is the language clear, i.e., are ideas expressed clearly and simply with appropriate word, phrase, and expression choices?*

    **Scale:** 1 (not at all) – 5 (very much)

---

14. **Creativity of Speech**  
    *On a scale from 1 = not at all to 5 = very much, what is the level of creativity in the speech, i.e., is it original while remaining appropriate in the speech context?*

    **Scale:** 1 (not at all) – 5 (very much)

---

## Global Evaluation and Comment

15. **Global Evaluation (Scrollbar)**  
    *Provide an overall score for the presentation on a scale from 1 to 100, where:*

    - 1 = the presentation is not acceptable at all  
    - 25 = quite poor  
    - 50 = neutral  
    - 75 = good but not final-round quality  
    - 100 = perfect, could win  

    **Response:** Numeric value from 1 to 100 (adjustable).

---

16. **Comment (Optional)**  
    *Leave a comment if you wish.*

    **Response type:** Open-text field
